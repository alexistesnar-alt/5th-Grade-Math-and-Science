Fueling Athletes

Pacing: 4 weeks

LS.5.1.1 · LS.5.1.2

The puzzle we will figure out: Athletes in action (Simone Biles, Katie Ledecky, Noah Lyles): how do the body's systems work together so an athlete can perform?
NC Standards

  • LS.5.1.2Use models to compare the major systems of the human body (digestive, respiratory, circulatory, muscular, skeletal, nervous) as it relates to their functions necessary for life.
