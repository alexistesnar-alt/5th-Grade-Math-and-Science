How the ocean changes North Carolina weather
  • Water heats up and cools down more slowly than land. So the coast has milder days and nights.
  • Sea breeze: on sunny afternoons the land gets hotter than the ocean, so cool air blows from the sea onto land. At night it flips, and a land breeze blows out to sea.
  • The Gulf Stream is a warm ocean current that flows past North Carolina. It keeps the coast warmer in winter and gives energy to storms.
  • Hurricanes form over warm ocean water. They get weaker as they move over land, like the mountains.
