5th Grade Math

Fluency Lab

Practice first. Then take the Skill Check. Fluency means you are accurate, efficient, and flexible.

How it works
  1. Pick a module and start at Level 1.
  2. Each set has 10 problems. Stuck? Tap Hint. Miss twice and you’ll see the steps.
  3. Get 9 or 10 right on the first try, then move up a level. A gold square means you mastered that level.
  4. When you’ve done the levels, take the Skill Check to show what you know.

Your progress saves on this device only.
