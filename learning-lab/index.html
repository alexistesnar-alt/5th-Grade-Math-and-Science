Learning Lab

Practice the skills 5th graders need to know quickly and accurately. Pick a skill, choose a level, and answer 10 questions. You get feedback after every answer.

How to grow your fluency

Fluency means you are accurate, you can do it efficiently, and you are flexible with strategies. Start with accuracy at level 1. When you get 9 or 10 right, move up a level. Try Beat the clock only after you are accurate.
