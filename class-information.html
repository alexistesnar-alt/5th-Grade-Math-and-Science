How We Learn Math

We use Illustrative Mathematics. Most lessons follow the same routine: a warm-up to get our brains going, one or two activities where students work on a problem together before anyone shows them how, a lesson synthesis where we share strategies and name the big idea, and a short cool-down so I can see what each student understands. Mistakes are part of learning, and we explain our thinking out loud and in writing.

How We Learn Science

Each science unit starts with a phenomenon, a real puzzle like why the Statue of Liberty turned green or why hemlock trees are dying. Students ask questions, investigate, build models and use evidence to explain what is happening.
