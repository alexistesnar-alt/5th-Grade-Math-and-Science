5th Grade Math · Unit 7

Shapes on the Coordinate Plane

Graphing points and patterns on the coordinate plane, line plots, and classifying quadrilaterals.

NC.5.OA.3 · NC.5.MD.2 · NC.5.G.1 · NC.5.G.3  |  May (about 11 days)

Learning Targets
  • I can graph and name points in the first quadrant.
  • I can make two number patterns and graph them.
  • I can sort quadrilaterals by their properties.
NC Standards
  • NC.5.OA.3Generate two numerical patterns from two given rules, find relationships between them, and graph the ordered pairs.
  • NC.5.MD.2Represent and interpret data, including line plots with fractions of a unit.
  • NC.5.G.1Graph points in the first quadrant of the coordinate plane and interpret ordered pairs in real-world problems.
  • NC.5.G.3Classify quadrilaterals in a hierarchy based on their properties.
