All 5th Grade Math Standards in Our Units
  • NC.5.OA.2Write, explain and evaluate numerical expressions with the four operations, using parentheses, to solve up to two-step problems.
  • NC.5.OA.3Generate two numerical patterns from two given rules, find relationships between them, and graph the ordered pairs.
  • NC.5.NBT.1Explain the patterns in place value: a digit is 10 times what it would be one place to its right and 1/10 of what it would be one place to its left; multiply and divide by powers of 10.
  • NC.5.NBT.3Read, write and compare decimals to the thousandths.
  • NC.5.NBT.5Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
  • NC.5.NBT.6Find quotients with remainders of whole numbers with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors.
  • NC.5.NBT.7Add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals to hundredths, and explain the reasoning.
  • NC.5.NF.1Add and subtract fractions, including mixed numbers, with unlike denominators.
  • NC.5.NF.3Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator, and solve word problems with it.
  • NC.5.NF.4Multiply a fraction by a whole number or a fraction, including mixed numbers, and find areas of rectangles with fraction side lengths.
  • NC.5.NF.7Divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions.
  • NC.5.MD.1Convert measurements within the same system (metric and customary) and solve multi-step problems.
  • NC.5.MD.2Represent and interpret data: collect data that changes over time, make and read a line graph, and decide whether a survey question gives categorical data, numerical data, or data that changes over time.
  • NC.5.MD.4Recognize volume as an attribute of solid figures and measure it by counting unit cubes.
  • NC.5.MD.5Relate volume to multiplication and addition: V = l × w × h and V = B × h, including volumes of figures made of two prisms.
  • NC.5.G.1Graph points in the first quadrant of the coordinate plane and interpret ordered pairs in real-world problems.
  • NC.5.G.3Classify quadrilaterals in a hierarchy based on their properties.

Standards summarized from the NC Standard Course of Study for Mathematics. Dates follow the BCS 2026–27 curriculum map and may shift.
