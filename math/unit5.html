5th Grade Math · Unit 5

Place Value Patterns and Decimal Operations

Place value to the thousandths, multiplying and dividing by powers of 10, and all four operations with decimals.

NC.5.NBT.1 · NC.5.NBT.3 · NC.5.NBT.7  |  Jan 13 – Apr 10

Learning Targets
  • I can read, write and compare decimals to the thousandths.
  • I can multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1,000.
  • I can add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals.
NC Standards
  • NC.5.NBT.1Explain the patterns in place value: a digit is 10 times what it would be one place to its right and 1/10 of what it would be one place to its left; multiply and divide by powers of 10.
  • NC.5.NBT.3Read, write and compare decimals to the thousandths.
  • NC.5.NBT.7Add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals to hundredths, and explain the reasoning.
