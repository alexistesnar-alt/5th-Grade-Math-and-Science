5th Grade Math · Unit 4

Wrapping Up Multiplication and Division with Multi-Digit Numbers

The standard algorithm for multiplication and dividing with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors.

NC.5.NBT.5 · NC.5.NBT.6 · NC.5.NF.3  |  Dec 2 – Jan 12

Learning Targets
  • I can multiply multi-digit numbers with the standard algorithm.
  • I can divide with up to a four-digit dividend and a two-digit divisor.
  • I can explain what a remainder means in a word problem.
NC Standards
  • NC.5.NBT.5Fluently multiply multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
  • NC.5.NBT.6Find quotients with remainders of whole numbers with up to four-digit dividends and two-digit divisors.
  • NC.5.NF.3Interpret a fraction as division of the numerator by the denominator, and solve word problems with it.
