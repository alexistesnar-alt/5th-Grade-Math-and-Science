Multiplying and Dividing Fractions

Multiplying fractions and mixed numbers, and dividing unit fractions and whole numbers.

NC.5.NF.4 · NC.5.NF.7  |  Oct 26 – Dec 1

Learning Targets
  • I can multiply fractions and mixed numbers.
  • I can find the area of a rectangle with fraction side lengths.
  • I can divide a unit fraction by a whole number and a whole number by a unit fraction.
NC Standards
  • NC.5.NF.4Multiply a fraction by a whole number or a fraction, including mixed numbers, and find areas of rectangles with fraction side lengths.
  • NC.5.NF.7Divide unit fractions by whole numbers and whole numbers by unit fractions.
