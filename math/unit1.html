5th Grade Math · Unit 1

Finding Volume

What volume is, finding it by counting cubes, and using V = l × w × h and V = B × h.

NC.5.MD.4 · NC.5.MD.5  |  Aug 31 – Sep 17

Learning Targets
  • I can find volume by counting unit cubes.
  • I can use V = l × w × h and V = B × h.
  • I can find the volume of a figure made of two prisms.
NC Standards
  • NC.5.MD.4Recognize volume as an attribute of solid figures and measure it by counting unit cubes.
  • NC.5.MD.5Relate volume to multiplication and addition: V = l × w × h and V = B × h, including volumes of figures made of two prisms.
