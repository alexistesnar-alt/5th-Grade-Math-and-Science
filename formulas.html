Formula and Equation Sheet

The math and science formulas we use in 5th grade. Print it or keep it open while you work.

Math
Volume (NC.5.MD.5)

V = l × w × h
V = B × h

B = area of the base = l × w. Volume is measured in cubic units (cm³, in³, ft³). For two prisms put together, find each volume and add.

Order of Operations (NC.5.OA.2)
  1. Parentheses ( )
  2. Multiply and divide, left to right
  3. Add and subtract, left to right

(6 + 4) × 3 = 10 × 3 = 30
6 + 4 × 3 = 6 + 12 = 18

Place Value (NC.5.NBT.1, NBT.3)
thousandshundredstensones.tenthshundredthsthousandths
1,000100101.0.10.010.001

Each place is 10 times the place to its right. × 10, × 100, × 1,000 moves digits 1, 2, 3 places to the left; ÷ moves them to the right. Example: 3.25 × 100 = 325.

Fractions (NC.5.NF.1, NF.3, NF.4, NF.7)

Fraction as division: 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4.
Add or subtract: find a common denominator first. 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
Multiply: multiply the numerators and the denominators. 2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15.
Whole number × fraction: 3 × 2/5 = 6/5 = 1 1/5.
Divide: 1/3 ÷ 4 = 1/12   and   4 ÷ 1/3 = 12.

Area of a Rectangle

A = l × w

Sides can be fractions: 1/2 ft × 3/4 ft = 3/8 ft².

Coordinate Plane (NC.5.G.1)

An ordered pair is (x, y). Start at the origin (0, 0), go over x, then up y. (3, 5): over 3, up 5.

Measurement Conversions (NC.5.MD.1)
Metric

1 kilometer (km) = 1,000 meters (m)
1 meter = 100 centimeters (cm)
1 centimeter = 10 millimeters (mm)
1 kilogram (kg) = 1,000 grams (g)
1 liter (L) = 1,000 milliliters (mL)

Customary

1 foot (ft) = 12 inches (in)
1 yard (yd) = 3 feet = 36 inches
1 mile (mi) = 5,280 feet
1 pound (lb) = 16 ounces (oz)
1 cup (c) = 8 fluid ounces
1 pint (pt) = 2 cups  ·  1 quart (qt) = 2 pints
1 gallon (gal) = 4 quarts

Time

1 minute = 60 seconds
1 hour = 60 minutes
1 day = 24 hours
1 week = 7 days

To change a larger unit to a smaller unit, multiply. To change a smaller unit to a larger unit, divide.

Science
Speed (PS.5.2.2)

speed = distance ÷ time

A cart rolls 12 meters in 4 seconds: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 meters per second.
distance = speed × time  ·  time = distance ÷ speed

Conservation of Mass (PS.5.1.1)

mass before = mass after

In a closed container the total mass stays the same, even in a chemical change. In an open container a gas can escape, so the scale shows less.

Body Organization (LS.5.1.1)

cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism

Energy in a Food Chain (LS.5.2.2)

sun → producer → consumer → consumer

Decomposers break down dead things and return nutrients to the soil.

Water Cycle (ESS.5.1.4)

evaporation → condensation → precipitation → collection

The sun's energy drives the cycle. Plants add water vapor by transpiration.

Weather Tools (ESS.5.1.1)

thermometer → temperature
barometer → air pressure
anemometer → wind speed
wind vane → wind direction
rain gauge → precipitation
